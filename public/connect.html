By design

What is HubSpot, and why is it here?

HubSpot is a CRM (customer relationship manager). Think of it as the studio's address book plus sales tracker: one record per person, with every call, note and follow-up attached, and every possible project tracked as a deal that moves through stages until it is won or lost.

The problem it solves

Our own database and dashboard answer "how is the phone system doing?" They are for Nikhil's numbers. But a lead does not end when the call ends. A designer visits, a quote is sent, the client thinks for three weeks, someone follows up. That story needs a home that the whole team can see and work in. That home is the CRM.

So the split is clean: the dashboard measures the assistant; HubSpot manages the relationships the assistant creates.

Without a CRM

  • Leads live in Telegram messages that scroll away
  • No single view of one customer across calls
  • No pipeline: nobody can say how many deals are open
  • Follow-ups depend on memory

With HubSpot

  • One record per customer; a repeat caller is recognised by phone number
  • Tasks remind the team to call back
  • Deals show the pipeline and what is won or lost
  • Any designer or manager can pick it up

Rules we keep: callers who are not a fit (closed) are not pushed to the CRM, so it stays clean. Prices are never written into it. If HubSpot is down, the call, the Telegram note and the dashboard still work.

Connect HubSpot in 5 steps

  1. Create a free HubSpot account at hubspot.com (free CRM).
  2. In HubSpot, click the gear icon (Settings) → Integrations → Private Apps → Create private app. Name it Aangan Desk.
  3. Open the Scopes tab and tick: crm.objects.contacts.read, crm.objects.contacts.write, crm.objects.deals.write, crm.objects.notes.write, crm.objects.tasks.write. Click Create app and copy the access token (it starts with pat-).
  4. Give the token to your builder (me) and it is added to Vercel as HUBSPOT_TOKEN.
  5. Come back to this page: the light on box 6 turns green. Press Test, then make a call and look for the contact in HubSpot.

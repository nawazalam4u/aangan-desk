THE NINE CHECKS · CASE 03 / NIKHIL
Aangan Studio, Pune · Unanswered enquiries · Build scope: incoming phone calls only
MESA · AI and its Application · Cohort C4

First principles: what must be true before we automate?

1. The jobAnswer every enquiry call, find out if it is worth a designer's time, and pass it on with everything already asked.
2. The facts~200 enquiries/month; ~48% unanswered in 48 h; ~33% outside 10am–7pm; 2-person desk; <1 h reply converts 4×; project ₹8–14 lakh.
3. The rules we must obeyqualified.md (5 criteria), services.md (scope, Pune/PCMC, 6-week lead time), pricing.md (no number may ever be quoted).
4. Where the human staysPricing, the consultation, complaints from existing clients, and the final fit decision.
#CheckEvidence from the caseResult
KILL SWITCHES · any NO = do not build
01Problem RealDocumented, ongoing pain?Nikhil pulled his own numbers: ~48% of ~200 enquiries a month get no response within 48 hours, and an answer within 1 hour converts 4× better than next day. Transcripts show it live: T08 missed at 10:47pm, T16 never logged, T07 left with no details taken.PASS
02Workflow RepeatedDoes it recur regularly?~200 enquiries every month; 20 of the 40 September transcripts are phone calls asking the same questions (what, where, size, when, who decides).PASS
03Input AvailableInputs exist and accessible now?The caller's own words on the call, plus enquiries/ (40 transcripts), services.md, pricing.md and qualified.md, all provided. The voice line is available through a voice-AI platform (Vaani: WebRTC now, phone number when bought).PASS
SIZING · any NO = build something smaller
04Output ValuableWould someone act on it?Designers act on a complete handoff note instead of "a forwarded message with no context"; Nikhil acts on the numbers he today pulls by hand; callers get an answer at any hour.PASS
05Impact MeasurableTrackable with a number?Time to answer, % answered within 5 minutes, qualified / closed / escalated counts, time for a designer to claim the lead, consultations booked, and cost per call, all logged per call on the dashboard.PASS
08ROI Worth ItBenefit clearly beats cost?Voice ~₹5.50/min plus ~₹0.4 of AI analysis per call; hosting, database and CRM on free tiers. At 200 calls a month this is a few thousand rupees, against an average project of ₹8–14 lakh: one extra won project pays for many months.PASS
BOUNDARY · any NO = build the tool, the human stays here
06Failure Risk OKIf the AI is wrong, what happens?Wrong qualification is recoverable: every closed call is logged and reviewable, and unclear calls go to a call-back. A wrong price is not recoverable: pricing.md says pricing is "indicative only, varies by site" and no number may be quoted; a figure on an ₹8–14 lakh decision anchors the customer.NO for pricing
07Judgment ProtectedHuman review before anything consequential?The AI only answers, collects and forwards. The designer claims the lead, runs the consultation and owns the price; senior staff own existing-client complaints (T09). Nothing is promised or quoted by the AI.PASS
09Owner ClearOne person accountable?Nikhil: he wrote qualified.md ("This is my rubric"), the rules in the system mirror it, and the dashboard lists every decision so he can correct the rubric.PASS
THE CUT: quoting per-square-foot pricing
Nikhil asked: "the system should be able to quote our standard per-square-foot pricing so people don't have to wait for that." Check 06 (Failure Risk) stops it, with 07 behind it: pricing.md forbids any number, range or "starts at" figure, and pricing is confirmed by the assigned designer at the consultation. What we built instead: the agent gives the approved line ("Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope; your designer will walk you through it at the consultation") and offers to book it; asking about price never disqualifies a caller. Code audits every transcript and alerts if a figure ever slips.
